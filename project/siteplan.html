Honest Auto Repair

A future workshop offering reliable service at fair prices.

Site Name

Honest Auto Repair – I chose this name because I enjoy repairing vehicles and want to create a workshop that offers excellent service at honest prices.

Optional domain: honestautorepairgt.com

Site Purpose

The site will showcase real photos of the workshop, provide location details with Google Maps, list services with prices, and allow customers to book appointments online. It will also include customer reviews and special offers to build trust and attract new clients.

Scenarios

  • How can I book an appointment for a brake inspection?
  • Where is the shop located and what are the business hours?
  • What services and prices are available for oil changes and tire rotations?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #004aad (Deep Blue) – used for headings, navigation, and accents.

Secondary Color: #f4a300 (Warm Orange) – used for buttons, highlights, and call‑to‑action elements.

Neutral Background: #2e2e2e (Dark Gray) – used for footer and background sections.

Typography

Heading Font: Roboto – clean and professional, used for titles and headings.

Body Font: Open Sans – easy to read, used for paragraphs and service descriptions.
